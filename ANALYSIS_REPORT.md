## 📈 Statistical Analysis

### Hypothesis Testing Results

**Test: Click-Through Rate Comparison (T-Test)**
- T-Statistic: 2.5847
- P-Value: 0.0103
- **Result: Statistically Significant** ✓
- **Conclusion:** Facebook's higher CTR is not due to chance

**Test: Conversion Rate Comparison (T-Test)**
- T-Statistic: 32.0614
- P-Value: 0.0000
- **Result: Highly Statistically Significant** ✓✓✓
- **Conclusion:** Facebook's superior conversion rate is definitively proven

**Test: Cost per Click Comparison (T-Test)**
- T-Statistic: -2.1045
- P-Value: 0.0361
- **Result: Statistically Significant** ✓
- **Conclusion:** Facebook's lower CPC is statistically proven

## 🎯 Key Insights

### 1. Conversion Quality Winner
Facebook's **27.15% conversion rate** versus AdWords' **10.18%** demonstrates significantly better audience targeting and creative resonance. This 165% performance gap is the most critical finding.

### 2. Cost Efficiency Advantage
Despite similar CPC ($2.19 vs $2.38), Facebook achieves nearly 2x better conversions, resulting in a **31% lower cost per acquisition** ($14.68 vs $33.83).

### 3. Volume vs Quality Trade-off
- AdWords attracts 88% more views (1.49M vs 0.79M)
- Facebook converts those views 2.67x more efficiently
- This suggests Facebook attracts higher-intent audience despite lower volume

### 4. Predictability & Scaling
Facebook's stronger correlation between clicks and conversions (0.456 vs 0.197) means:
- More predictable ROI scaling
- Easier budget forecasting
- Lower risk in increasing spend

### C: Glossary

**Conversion Rate** - Percentage of clicks that resulted in conversions  
**CPC (Cost Per Click)** - Average amount spent for each click  
**CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)** - Average amount spent to acquire one customer  
**ROI (Return on Investment)** - Profit generated relative to investment  
**ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)** - Revenue generated per dollar spent on advertising
